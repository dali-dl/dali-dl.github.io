Da Li

Associate Professor in AI at the University of York

My research interests include embodied AI, world models, and multimodal learning. My research aims to build intelligent systems that can learn and adapt through interactions with the physical world.

Previously, I was a Senior Research Scientist and Project Lead at Samsung AI Center Cambridge. I received my doctoral training at Queen Mary University of London (QMUL) and Surrey’s Centre for Vision, Speech and Signal Processing (CVSSP). Prior to moving to the UK, I worked as a Computer Vision Engineer at West-well Lab (AI Unicorn) and a 3D Software Engineer at Autodesk R&D.

SAINTS CDT studentships

Safe AI

SAINTS is York’s UKRI Centre for Doctoral Training in lifelong safety assurance of AI-enabled autonomous systems. Relevant topics include safe self-evolving embodied AI, uncertainty-aware world models, and lifelong adaptation.

Applications normally open between November and January. Students interested in working with me are welcome to discuss research fit before applying through the formal SAINTS process.
